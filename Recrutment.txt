# RECRUITMENT – Nexus Studio  
## Join the adventure!

Thank you for your interest in **Nexus Studio** – a free, browser‑based WYSIWYG website editor powered by Gemini AI.

We are looking for **volunteers, testers, developers, designers, and content creators** to help us improve the tool and spread the word.

---

## 🔍 Who we’re looking for

| Role | What you can do |
|------|----------------|
| **Early testers / users** | Use the tool, report bugs, suggest improvements, share your feedback. |
| **Developers (JS, HTML/CSS, Python)** | Enhance the editor, add features (drag & drop, new templates, AI integrations, export options). |
| **UI/UX designers** | Propose visual and ergonomic improvements. |
| **Technical writers / community managers** | Write tutorials, blog posts, manage social media, create video demos. |
| **Ambassadors** | Share Nexus Studio in your communities (dev.to, LinkedIn, Twitter, Product Hunt, Discord). |

---

## 📌 How to apply

Send a message to: **nexus-studio@example.com** (replace with your real email)  
or open a **GitHub issue** with the label `recruitment`.

Please include:
- Your name / handle
- What you would like to do (tester, developer, design, writing, etc.)
- Your experience (even beginners are welcome – enthusiasm matters!)
- Availability (a few hours per week, occasional, etc.)

---

## 🎁 What we offer

- Be part of a useful open‑source project
- Credits in the repository and on the official website
- Early access to new features
- Possibility to co‑author articles or present the tool at online events
